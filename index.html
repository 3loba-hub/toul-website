Our Story

Not Just a Scent — a Welcome

In Sudanese homes, bakhoor is how a room says hello. It's lit for guests arriving, for brides on their wedding day, for the quiet hour after coffee when the house finally settles. TOUL — تُل — was born from that ritual: bakhoor blends, perfume oils, and dilka made the way our mothers and grandmothers made them, now carried from Khartoum to Cairo, and soon further still.
